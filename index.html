How it works

  
    
    
  1. Choose a concept and options. The prompt updates as you change them.
    2. 
    
  2. Copy the prompt and paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
    3. 
    
  3. Save the HTML file it returns and open it in Chrome. Preview it at 1–4× speed.
    4. 
    
  4. Voice-over: click Copy timed script, record or generate audio from it, then load it into the player or merge it in your editor. To fix the timings to your own audio instead, choose Lock to my voice-over above.
    5. 
    
  5. Download the MP4 (or a silent video plus the merge command) and upload it.
    6.
